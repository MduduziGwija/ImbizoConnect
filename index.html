    The story

    From a 2015 high school idea to a working concept

    In 2015, two high school friends, Mduduzi Gwija and Karabo Kennedy Mafaralala, kept coming back to the same question. Why does applying to university mean filling in the same forms again and again, paying a fee at every institution, and guessing whether your marks are good enough?

    That conversation became an idea, and the idea stayed. Years later, Mduduzi Gwija conceptualised and built ImbizoConnect: one profile, every public university, and a clear picture of your chances before you pay. Imbizo means a gathering, and this is one: every institution, programme, fee and deadline in one place.

    Conceptualised by Mduduzi Gwija, with thanks to Karabo Kennedy Mafaralala for the conversations that started it.

    Paying for your studies

    An application fee is the smallest cost. Here's how most students fund tuition, residence and books.

    Government

    NSFAS bursary

    Covers tuition, residence, meals, books and transport at public universities and TVET colleges for SA citizens whose combined household income is R350 000 a year or less (R600 000 for students with disabilities).

    • Apply separately from your university application
    • Applications for open in the last quarter of the year
    • You'll need your ID, your parents' IDs and proof of income
    nsfas.org.za ↗
    Teaching

    Funza Lushaka

    Full bursary for BEd students training to teach priority subjects such as Maths, Science, Technology and African languages. In return, you teach at a public school for one year for every year funded.

    funzalushaka.doe.gov.za ↗
    Missing middle

    ISFAP & university aid

    If your household earns too much for NSFAS but can't afford fees, look at ISFAP, university merit awards and financial aid offices. Most universities also discount or waive the application fee on request if you can show financial need.

    isfap.co.za ↗
    Private

    Company bursaries

    Banks, mining houses, SOEs and engineering firms fund scarce-skills degrees, and many offer a job afterwards. Most close between March and September of your matric year, so start early.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I apply before I get my final matric results?

    Yes. That's how it normally works. Universities make provisional offers based on your Grade 11 final results and confirm them against your Grade 12 NSC results in January.

    What is the CAO?

    The Central Applications Office handles undergraduate applications for UKZN, DUT, MUT and UNIZULU. One fee (R250 for SA applicants) covers up to six programme choices across all four, so ImbizoConnect only charges it once in your total.

    Is the application fee refundable?

    Almost never. That's why ImbizoConnect shows your eligibility before you pay. If you're far below a programme's cut-off, you can save the fee.

    Why is my APS different on a university's website?

    Several universities use their own method. Wits gives up to 8 points per subject, UCT uses a Faculty Points Score, SU uses an average percentage, and some count Life Orientation. We use the standard 7-point scale as a common baseline and flag the differences on each university card.

    What's a Bachelor's, Diploma or Higher Certificate pass?

    Bachelor's pass: 40% in Home Language, 50% in four other designated subjects and 30% in the rest. Diploma pass: 40% in Home Language, 40% in four other subjects and 30% in the rest. Higher Certificate pass: 40% in Home Language, 40% in two others and 30% in three more. You need a Bachelor's pass for degree study.
